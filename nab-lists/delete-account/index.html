You can delete your Nab Lists account and associated data in either of these ways:

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Nab Lists and sign in with Google
  2. On the home screen, open the menu (⋮)
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm

That removes your profile, signs you out, deletes lists you own, and removes you from lists you joined.

By email

If you can’t use the app, email:

dan+nablists@schormans.com

Include the Google account email you used to sign in. We aim to complete deletion within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your Nab Lists user profile (name, email, photo URL)
  • Lists you own (and their invite codes)
  • Your membership on lists you joined
  • Your Firebase Authentication account for Nab Lists

This does not delete your Google account itself — only the Nab Lists app account and data tied to it.
