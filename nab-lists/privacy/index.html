Nab Lists (“the App”) is a shared shopping list application. This policy explains what data we collect and how it is used.

Who we are

Nab Lists is operated by Dan Schormans. Contact: dan+nablists@schormans.com.

Data we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive:

  • Your Google account user ID
  • Display name
  • Email address
  • Profile photo URL (if available)

When you use the App, we store:

  • Shopping lists you create or join
  • List items (text, checked state)
  • Membership of lists (who has access)
  • Invite codes used to share lists
  • Timestamps related to the above

How we use data

We use this data only to:

  • Authenticate you
  • Sync and share shopping lists between people you invite
  • Operate and improve the App

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your shopping list contents for advertising.

Storage and processors

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), operated by Google. Google’s processing is subject to their terms and privacy policy:

  • Firebase privacy
  • Google privacy policy

Sharing

List contents and membership are visible to other members of that list (people who join with an invite code you share). Do not share invite codes with people you do not trust.

Retention and deletion

  • You can remove items and delete lists you own from within the App.
  • You can sign out at any time.
  • To request deletion of your account data, email dan+nablists@schormans.com. We will delete your user profile and remove you from lists where feasible.

Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the App if you are under 13.

Security

Access to lists requires Google Sign-In. Server-side security rules restrict list and item access to list members. No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
