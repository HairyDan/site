death metal logo generator

i've always thought it would be funny to make one of these. give it a name, grow some thorns, then keep going until nobody can read it.

a–z, numbers, spaces, &, hyphens and apostrophes. up to 24 characters.

growing…

exports are transparent unless you tick background. svg keeps the actual paths; png is 2400 × 1240. the same name, seed and sliders give the same logo.

what's the maths?

each letter starts as a few lines. a sum of sine waves bends those lines, and variable-width outlines give them some weight. no fonts to download, or to go missing when you open the svg.

thorns grow out of the letter strokes. each one follows a curved path, gets thinner towards its tip, and can grow smaller branches of its own. reach changes their length; thorns changes how many get to grow.

webbing joins nearby points on different branches and letters with thin quadratic curves. a spatial grid keeps that search local. turn it down if the name has completely disappeared.

ornament symmetry reflects the extra outer branches. turning it down lets the two sides drift apart. it leaves the letters facing the right way, which is possibly a bit cowardly.

all the random choices come from the seed. nothing runs once you've stopped moving the sliders.
