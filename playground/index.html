playground

tiny toys & experiments. all vanilla javascript.

my dissertation at southampton was on modelling slime mould in java, mostly trying to work out how simple cells end up making useful networks. then i gave them better food sensing and made two colonies compete, which mostly made a mess of things. still a bit obsessed with emergent complexity, so i've brought some of that back into the slime experiment.
